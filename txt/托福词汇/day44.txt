outstanding |英 [aʊt'stændɪŋ]  美 ['aʊt'stændɪŋ]| adj. 杰出的；显著的；未解决的；未偿付的/n. 未偿贷款/
The company is an outstanding example of a small business that grew into a big one.该公司是小企业成长为大公司的突出例子。
We still have some outstanding issues to resolve before we'll have a treaty that is ready to sign.我们在签署这份条约之前还有一些悬而未决的问题要解决。
The total debt outstanding is $70 billion.尚未偿还的总债务额是700亿美元。
Derartu is an outstanding athlete and deserved to win.德拉图是位杰出的运动员，他理应获胜。


The cargo of a ship or plane is the goods that it is carrying. 货物
cargo |英 ['kɑːgəʊ]  美 ['kɑrɡo]| n. 货物，船货/n. (Cargo)人名；(英、西)卡戈/
The boat calls at the main port to load its regular cargo of bananas.船停泊在那个大港，装载其常规货物香蕉。


If a doctor or a nurse administers a drug, they give it to a patient. (医生、护士) 派发 (药物)
If someone administers something such as a country, the law, or a test, they take responsibility for organizing and supervising it. 监管 (国家、法律、考试等)
administer |英 [əd'mɪnɪstə]  美 [əd'mɪnɪstɚ]| vt. 管理；执行；给予/vi. 给予帮助；执行遗产管理人的职责；担当管理人/
The physician may prescribe but not administer the drug.内科医师可以开处方但不可发药。
The plan calls for the UN to administer the country until elections can be held.该计划呼吁联合国监管该国直至选举可以举行。


inferiority 自卑
Inferior is also a noun. (能力或地位) 低于他人者; 部下
If one person is regarded as inferior to another, they are regarded as less important because they have less status or ability. 低等的; 次要的
Something that is inferior is not as good as something else. 较差的
inferior |英 [ɪn'fɪərɪə]  美 [ɪn'fɪrɪɚ]| adj. 差的；自卑的；下级的，下等的/n. 下级；次品/
I found it difficult to shake off a sense of social inferiority.我发现很难摆脱社会地位低下的自卑感。
It was a gentleman's duty always to be civil, even to his inferiors.一个绅士的责任是一直保持彬彬有礼，即使是对比自己地位低的人。
He preferred the company of those who were intellectually inferior to himself.他喜欢与那些智力不如他的人为伍。
This resulted in overpriced and often inferior products.这就导致产品标价过高而往往质量较次。
The cassettes were of inferior quality.这些盒式磁带质量较差。
