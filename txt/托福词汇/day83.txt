If you shuffle playing cards, you mix them up before you begin a game. 洗 (牌)
If you shuffle around, you move your feet about while standing or you move your bottom about while sitting, often because you feel uncomfortable or embarrassed. (因不舒服或尴尬) 站着的脚来回挪动; 坐立不安
