The outline of something is its general shape, especially when it cannot be clearly seen. 轮廓
You say that an object is outlined when you can see its general shape because there is light behind it. 映衬出…的轮廓
An outline is a general explanation or description of something. 概要
If you outline an idea or a plan, you explain it in a general way. 概述

If someone negates something, they say that it does not exist. 否定
If one thing negates another, it causes that other thing to lose the effect or value that it had. 使无效
negate |英 [nɪ'geɪt]  美 [nɪˈɡet]| vt. 否定；取消；使无效/vi. 否定；否认；无效/n. 对立面；反面/

These weaknesses negated his otherwise progressive attitude towards the staff.这些缺点抹杀了他本来对工作人员的开明态度。


If one thing happens and then another thing happens in consequence or as a consequence, the second thing happens as a result of the first. 结果
The consequences of something are the results or effects of it. 结果; 后果
consequence |英 ['kɒnsɪkw(ə)ns]  美 ['kɑnsəkwɛns]| n. 结果；重要性；推论/
Maternity services were to be reduced as a consequence of falling birth rates.孕产服务因下降的出生率而将被削减。
His death was totally unexpected and, in consequence, no plans had been made for his replacement.他的死纯粹出乎意料，因此还没有为他的替代人定出计划。
Her lawyer said she understood the consequences of her actions and was prepared to go to jail.她的律师说她明白自己行为的后果并准备去坐牢。
