Something that is below the belt is cruel and unfair. 不公正的
If you belt somewhere, you move or travel there very fast. 飞奔
Belt is also a noun. 狠揍; 猛打
If someone belts you, they hit you very hard. If someone belts something, they hit it very hard. 狠揍; 猛打
A belt of land or sea is a long, narrow area of it that has some special feature. 狭长地带
A belt in a machine is a circular strip of rubber that is used to drive moving parts or to move objects along. 输送带
A belt is a strip of leather or cloth that you fasten around your waist. 腰带; 皮带
