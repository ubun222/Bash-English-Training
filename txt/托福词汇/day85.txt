If you make a splash, you become noticed or become popular because of something that you have done. 引起关注
If a magazine or newspaper splashes a story, it prints it in such a way that it is very noticeable. (报刊) 以显眼方式刊登
A splash of colour is an area of a bright colour which contrasts strongly with the colours around it. (与周围颜色形成鲜明对比的) 亮色块
A splash of a liquid is a small quantity of it that falls on something or is added to something. (少量液体的) 溅落; (少量液体的) 添加
A splash is the sound made when something hits water or falls into it. 拍打水的声音; 落入水的声音
If you splash a liquid somewhere or if it splashes, it hits someone or something and scatters in a lot of small drops. 使泼溅; 溅落
If you splash around or splash about in water, you hit or disturb the water in a noisy way, causing some of it to fly up into the air. 泼打戏水
