If something grazes another thing, it touches that thing lightly as it passes by. 擦过
A graze is a small wound caused by scraping against something. 擦伤
If you graze a part of your body, you injure your skin by scraping against something. 擦破皮
When animals graze or are grazed, they eat the grass or other plants that are growing in a particular place. You can also say that a field is grazed by animals. 放牧 (牛、羊等); (牛、羊等) 吃草
