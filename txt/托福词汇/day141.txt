If something unpleasant haunts you, you keep thinking or worrying about it over a long period of time. (令人不愉快的事) 萦绕在心头
haunt |英 [hɔːnt]  美 [hɔnt]| vt. 常出没于…；萦绕于…；经常去…/vi. 出没；作祟/n. 栖息地；常去的地方/
His ghost is said to haunt some of the rooms, banging a toy drum.据说他的鬼魂常在其中一些屋子里出现，还敲打着一个玩具鼓。
The islands are a favourite summer haunt for yachtsmen.这片岛屿是游艇驾驶者夏天最爱去的一个地方。
The stigma of being a bankrupt is likely to haunt him for the rest of his life.作为一名破产者的耻辱很可能在他的余生不断来纠缠他。
He would always be haunted by that scene in Well Park.他将不断回想起威尔公园的那一幕。

If something deceives you, it gives you a wrong impression and makes you believe something that is not true. 误导
If you deceive someone, you make them believe something that is not true, usually in order to get some advantage for yourself. 欺骗

When leaves, shoots, or plants sprout somewhere, they grow there. (叶子、芽、植株) 长出
When plants, vegetables, or seeds sprout, they produce new shoots or leaves. (植物、蔬菜、种子) 发芽

You use in theory to say that although something is supposed to be true or to happen in the way stated, it may not in fact be true or happen in that way. 从理论上说
The theory of a practical subject or skill is the set of rules and principles that form the basis of it. 基础理论
If you have a theory about something, you have your own opinion about it which you cannot prove but which you think is true. 观点
A theory is a formal idea or set of ideas that is intended to explain something. 理论
theory |英 ['θɪərɪ]  美 ['θiəri]| n. 理论；原理；学说；推测/
Achieving these goals is relatively easy in theory, yet quite difficult in practice.实现这些目标从理论上说比较容易，但在实践上却相当困难。
He taught us music theory.他教我们乐理。
There was a theory that he wanted to marry her.有种观点认为他想娶她。
Marx produced a new theory about historical change based upon conflict between competing groups.马克思提出了一个新理论，认为历史变革是由于对立集团的冲突而产生的。
