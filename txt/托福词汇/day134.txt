If organs or tissues regenerate or if something regenerates them, they heal and grow again after they have been damaged. 使再生; 再生
regeneration 重建; 复兴
To regenerate something means to develop and improve it to make it more active, successful, or important, especially after a period when it has been getting worse. 重建; 复兴
